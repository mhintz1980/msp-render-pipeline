## Facts worth not re-deriving

- **Run tests with `./.venv/Scripts/python.exe`** — bare `python` lacks
  `jsonschema` and silently drops 16 tests. Under the venv: 50/50.
- **The proof renders at 900×625 / 48 samples** (`verify_scene.py:307`), not the
  job's 1800×1250. Every composite judged so far is the half-res proof image.
- **Orange peel cannot show at this framing, and lighting will not change that.**
  Peel is 1–2 mm; the proof gets ~4 mm/px, production ~2 mm/px — sub-pixel.
  Measured: the same shader at 16.7 mm / strength 1.0 gives obvious stucco, at
  1.5 mm nothing; undenoised at 256 samples does not recover it. Mark's own
  photograph shows no peel at this framing either. A prior explanation told him
  sunlit renders are useful for checking orange peel — they are not. Lighting
  reveals roughness, highlights, bevels and colour.
- **Chroma was the real gap, and exposure fixed it, not AgX removal.** Saturation
  over a fixed rear-panel crop: 0.685 (AgX/−0.15) → 0.903 (Standard/−0.15, but
  18.3% channels clipped) → 0.983 (AgX/−1.5, no clipping). AgX stays.
- `MSP_YELLOW_PAINT` lives in `cad/RL300-SAFE-photoreal.blend`.
  `apply_powder_coat()` edits it in place from `photoreal.powder_coat`; it
  deliberately avoids `apply_livery_materials()`, which would replace all 11 CAD
  materials with two.
- `inject_bevel_shading` skips materials that already have a BEVEL node, which is
  why the job's `radius_m` never reached the body before `c0869b3`.
- **Residual T04 debt:** `mask_checks` is validated when present but not required
  by the report schema, so an independent producer could omit it. Product-plus-
  shadow coverage stays sensitive to the denoised shadow edge near mask byte 8.
